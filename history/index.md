## Historically the staff posted in laboratory have been struggling to get the pay parity with other scientific organization's scientific support staff. 
See the efforts made by BISLEA's teams and members over the years, from the archives of BISLEA. 

### Creation of posts of LTO-A & LTO-B in BIS Laboratories 

1.	In 1989, Cadre Review committee recommended that two levels beyond TS(Lab) should be created to remove stagnation in the Laboratory Technical cadre posts.
2.	In 1992, High Power Study Group constituted by Joint Ministerial Task Force for Strengthening the National Standards Body (BIS), Recommended in its Report at Clause 4.2.1.4, Page 25 that there should TWO Additional Levels of Group-A posts namely Laboratory Technical Officer-(A), LTO (A) in Pay Scale of Rs. 2200 – 4000 and Laboratory Technical Officer-(B), LTO (B) in Pay Scale of Rs. 3000 – 4500 be introduced in the Laboratory Technical Cadre Posts to provide suitable Career Progression Opportunities to the employees in this Cadre.
3.	In 1994, Cadre Review Committee Recommended that TWO HIGHER LEVELS beyond TS (Lab) should be created to improve Promotional Avenues and to remove stagnation in the Laboratory Technical Cadre Posts.
4.	In 1995, SSC constituted a Sub-Committee headed by Prof. E.S.R. Gopal, Director, NPL to examine the PROPOSAL for creation of two higher levels in the Laboratory Technical Cadre Posts. The Sub-Committee Recommended 12 Posts of Lab Technical Officer (A) in the scale of Rs.8000-275-13500 and 5 Posts of Lab Technical Officer (B) in the scale of Rs.10000-325-15200.
5.	On 5 Aug 1999, SSC approved the Recommendations of Prof. E.S.R. Gopal Sub-Committee.
6.	On 18-19 Aug 1999, EC 51st Meeting approved the Proposals of creation of two higher levels in the Laboratory Technical Cadre Posts. The Proposals were sent to Central Govt for approval.
7.	On 17 July 2000, The Ministry of Consumer Affairs & Public Distribution informed BIS to amend the BIS Regulations for making provisions of the LTO (A) & LTO (B).
8.	BIS submitted the proposals to SSC along with the amendments in the BIS Regulations for making provisions of the LTO (A) & LTO (B).
9.	On 27 Aug 2001, SSC Approved the Proposals along with the amendments in the BIS Regulations for making provisions of the LTO (A) & LTO (B). The SSC-Recommendations and amended Regulations along with Financial Implications were submitted to EC for approval.
10.	On 29 April 2002, EC directed for Work Study for Man Power requirement in the Laboratory and to examine whether the Lab is a Cost Centre or Profit Centre in BIS and what is the contribution of Lab in the earnings of BIS revenues. DDGL was asked to examine and submit the report.
11.	On 22 March 2004, DDGL had submitted the report with recommendations that there is shortage of Man Power in BIS Laboratories and Lab is a Profit Centre in the earnings of BIS revenues.
12.	On 31 August 2004, DDGA sent back the Work Study Report to DDGL for re-examination. DDGL submitted the Report after review.
13.	On 14 September 2005, EC 68th Meeting approved the amendments in the BIS Regulations for making provisions of the LTO (A) & LTO (B).
14.	The amendments in the BIS Regulations for making provisions of the LTO (A) & LTO (B) were sent to Central Govt for approval. The Ministry of Consumer Affairs & Public Distribution asked BIS to review the residency period for promotion in the amended BIS Regulations.
15.	On 21 December 2006, BIS Management finalised the residency period for promotion to STA, TS, LTO(A) & LTO(B) after consultation with Laboratory Representatives and decided to sent to Ministry for approval.
