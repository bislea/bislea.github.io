## BISLEA through its members have fought legal battles on multiple issues apart from main issue i.e. pay parity with other scientific organization's scientific support staff. 

Efforts made by BISLEA's teams and members over the years, from the archives of BISLEA are accessible here. Orders have been downloaded from Supreme court and Delhi High court's website. Some useful service matters cases would also be listed here. Advocates working for BISLEA or members may request to folder for educational purposes. 

### Pay scale of 1640 Rs case WP (C) 3925 of 1991 in Delhi High court  
1. BISLEA have fought a legal battle for pay parity with staff joining the BIS as Assistant posts for its members working as Technical Assistants(Labs).
2. Though most of members who fought this have retired we know about this by 1640 scale case. Data in digital formats is not available with current team.
3.	Delhi high court had ordered in the favor of the BIS laboratory employees they and were supposed to get pay arrears.
4.	Status- Disposed, [Judgement](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1LhIx4leXkkMeFRpT3D05VcYdFYOWJQMH/view?usp=sharing)

### Pay scale of 1640 Rs case Letter Patent Appeal (LPA) 958 of 2002 in Division bench of Delhi High court
1.	Delhi high court had ordered in the favor of the BIS laboratory employees they and were supposed to get pay arrears.
2.	BIS management had appealed against the judgement hence BISLEA is respondent here, however the result came in favor of laboratory employees.
3.	Status- Disposed, [Judgement summary](https://www.casemine.com/judgement/in/56090beee4b0149711175bf2)

### Pay scale of 1640 Rs case CIVIL APPEAL NO(s). 5185 OF 2007 in Supreme Court of India
1.	Though most of members who fought this have retired we know about this by 1640 scale case. Data in digital formats is not available with current team.
2.	BIS had filed case against the BISLEA and BISLEA had won in Supreme court, when department challenged the high court judgment came in favor of laboratory employees.
3.	The members were issued pay arrears in this matter.
4.	Status- Disposed, [Judgement PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1U17mUE7IdK_xZn_QC_TTHsnQ1yQWCDbA/view?usp=sharing)

### Insurance implementation case- WP (C) 5960 of 2023 in Delhi High Court

### Against arbitrary blocking of outside posts applications of employees WP (C) 13810 of 2024 in Delhi High Court 
1.	BIS management have blocked multiple outside post applications of employees citing shortage of staff, be it application to UPSC/ SSC/ State PSC or application by SC/ST candidates hence BISLEA have approached the DHC for justice. 
2.	[Request access to the documents (Petition copy available)](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1JQVv2HnS8QDqyJQgwbQKSpgu4Sw4OAnq?usp=drive_link)
3.	Status- Counter affidavit filed by BIS, BISLEA rejoinder filed.
