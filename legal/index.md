## BISLEA through its members have fought legal battles on multiple issues apart from main issue i.e. pay parity with other scientific organization's scientific support staff. 

See the efforts made by BISLEA's teams and members over the years, from the archives of BISLEA. Orders have been downloaded from Supreme court and Delhi High court's website. Some more useful cases have been listed here. Advocates working for BISLEA or members may request to folder for educational purposes. 

### 1640 case 
1.	BISLEA have fought a legal battle for pay parity with staff joining the BIS as Assistant posts for its members working as Technical Assistants(Labs).
2.	BISLEA have won in Supreme court.

#### Insurance implementation case- Brief WP C 5960 of 2023
1.	BISLEA have tried for implementation of accidental insurance as well as group savings linked insurance for its members, where for this we have approached Delhi High court also.
2.	[Request access to the documents(Including Petition)](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/193ZztRNw5cnRqlNQMQPcAYClZal3crc0?usp=drive_link)
3.	Status-Case is ongoing at the time of writing of this page on 16-11-2026, see the DHC website for latest update.
