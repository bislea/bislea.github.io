## Legal battles fought by BISLEA

BISLEA, through its members, has fought legal battles on multiple issues apart from the main issue, i.e. pay parity with the scientific support staff of other scientific organizations.

The efforts made by BISLEA's teams and members over the years are accessible here from the BISLEA archives. Orders have been downloaded from the websites of the Supreme Court and the Delhi High Court. Some useful service matter cases will also be listed here. Advocates working for BISLEA or its members may request access to the case folders for educational purposes.

### Pay scale of Rs. 1640 case: WP (C) 3925 of 1991 in the Delhi High Court
1. BISLEA fought a legal battle for its members working as Technical Assistants (Labs), seeking pay parity with staff joining BIS in Assistant posts.
2. Though most of the members who fought this case have retired, we know of it as the 1640 scale case. Data in digital format is not available with the current team.
3. The Delhi High Court ruled in favour of the BIS laboratory employees, and they were to receive pay arrears.
4. Status: Disposed. [Judgement](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1LhIx4leXkkMeFRpT3D05VcYdFYOWJQMH/view?usp=sharing)

### Pay scale of Rs. 1640 case: Letters Patent Appeal (LPA) 958 of 2002 before the Division Bench of the Delhi High Court
1. BIS management appealed to the Division Bench against the judgement in WP (C) 3925 of 1991, which had gone in favour of the BIS laboratory employees. BISLEA was therefore the respondent here.
2. The result of the appeal also came in favour of the laboratory employees.
3. Status: Disposed. [Judgement summary](https://www.casemine.com/judgement/in/56090beee4b0149711175bf2)

### Pay scale of Rs. 1640 case: Civil Appeal No(s). 5185 of 2007 in the Supreme Court of India
1. After the Division Bench judgement went against it, BIS filed an appeal against BISLEA in the Supreme Court. BISLEA won in the Supreme Court as well.
2. Ultimately, the appeal was disposed of by the Supreme Court, and the members/lab cadre employees were paid arrears in this matter.
3. Status: Disposed. [Judgement PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1U17mUE7IdK_xZn_QC_TTHsnQ1yQWCDbA/view?usp=sharing)

### Insurance implementation case: WP (C) 5960 of 2023 in the Delhi High Court
1. BISLEA has sought the implementation of accidental insurance as well as group savings-linked insurance for its members, and has approached the Delhi High Court for this.
2. [Request access to the case folder (includes petition copy)](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/193ZztRNw5cnRqlNQMQPcAYClZal3crc0?usp=drive_link)
3. Status (as of 16-11-2026): Counter affidavit filed by BIS; rejoinder filed by BISLEA. See the DHC website for updates.

### Self-appraisal in APAR for lab cadre, representation disposal case: WP (C) 11681 of 2023 in the Delhi High Court
1. BISLEA has sought the implementation of self-appraisal in the APAR. We had to approach the Delhi High Court for the disposal of our representation.
2. [Request access to the case folder (petition copy not included)](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1GyAcw06BNjSzmmAmjDqisL_n89XL7pqa?usp=drive_link)
3. Status: Disposed. [Judgement PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1UxkW-npbQsM1Jr28FwSrGnrn05seiPKw/view?usp=sharing)

### Promotion matter case: WP (C) 12121 of 2023 in the Delhi High Court

### Implementation of self-appraisal in APAR from 2024: WP (C) 5151 of 2024 in the Delhi High Court
1. BISLEA had to approach the court once again for the implementation of self-appraisal from the ongoing exercise. The result came in favour of BISLEA.
2. [Request access to the case folder (petition copy not included)](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1jZ_qAaYys-lJB6IcYl-bptBh-ir-DzY9?usp=drive_link)

### Premature increment case: WP (C) 10637 of 2024 in the Delhi High Court
1. Through this case, BISLEA has sought the grant of premature increments to its members as per the provisions of FR 27.
2. [Request access to the case folder (petition copy not included)](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1VE8cSkipfeUwbJAonWLSe9UyzGoGyFtW?usp=drive_link)
3. Status (as of 16-11-2026): Counter affidavit filed by BIS management on 28-10-2026.

### Employees' applications for outside posts case: WP (C) 13810 of 2024 in the Delhi High Court
1. BIS management has blocked multiple applications by employees for outside posts, citing a shortage of staff, be it applications to UPSC/SSC/State PSCs or applications by SC/ST candidates. BISLEA has challenged this before the Delhi High Court.
2. [Request access to the case folder (includes petition copy)](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1JQVv2HnS8QDqyJQgwbQKSpgu4Sw4OAnq?usp=drive_link)
3. Status (as of 16-11-2026): Counter affidavit filed by BIS; rejoinder filed by BISLEA.
